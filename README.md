# SmartClass Sierra Leone

## Project Overview

SmartClass Sierra Leone is a responsive educational landing page developed to support **Sustainable Development Goal 4 (Quality Education)** in Sierra Leone.

The project focuses on improving access to education through digital learning opportunities, scholarships, educational resources, and community support systems for students across the country.

---

# Product Name

## SmartClass Sierra Leone

---

# Problem Statement

Education remains one of the major challenges in Sierra Leone. Many children and young people face difficulties accessing quality education because of:

- Limited digital learning platforms
- Poor access to educational materials
- Financial challenges
- School dropout rates
- Limited internet and technology access
- Shortage of educational support systems

Many students especially in rural communities do not have equal access to modern learning opportunities and online educational resources.

This project was created to help promote digital education awareness and encourage equal access to quality learning opportunities for all students in Sierra Leone.
